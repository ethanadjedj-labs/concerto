## English

I just shipped Concerto — a tool that gives Claude Code developers a persistent, browser-accessible workspace in the cloud.

The problem it solves: Claude Code is powerful, but it's tied to your local machine. Every device switch means reconfiguring. Every closed laptop means a dead agent.

Concerto provisions a server (your cloud or ours), installs Claude Code, and gives you a browser terminal + MCP connector for claude.ai — in about 5 minutes. Close your laptop. Your agent keeps running.

Two plans: Hosted at $39/mo (fully managed) or BYOC at $99 one-time (deploys into your own DigitalOcean account).

Concerto sits squarely in the Anthropic ecosystem — built for Claude Max subscribers who want always-on agents without infrastructure overhead. If that's you or someone you know: concerto.run.

Feedback welcome.
