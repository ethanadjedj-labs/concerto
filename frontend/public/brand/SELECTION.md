# Logo Concept Selection

## Four concepts evaluated

**Concept 1 — Orbital Mark**: Three concentric arcs with staggered gaps (45°, 195°, 330°), each
carrying a small "agent" dot. A hub dot sits at center. Colors progress peach → mid-violet → violet
from inner to outer ring.

**Concept 2 — Conductor's Gesture**: A single sweeping cubic-bezier arc with a peach-to-violet
gradient — minimal, Anthropic-adjacent. Baton-tip dot at the end of the stroke.

**Concept 3 — Constellation**: Six nodes connected by faint lines forming a custom star-chart.
A larger hub node (cream) represents the operator/conductor; five outer nodes are agents.

**Concept 4 — Serif C + Orbital Tail**: A thick-stroke C letterform in peach with a thin violet
orbital arc extending from its base — the C being "conducted" by something orbiting it.

---

## Winner: Concept 1 — Orbital Mark

### Evaluation

**Distinctiveness at 32×32 favicon**
Three arcs with staggered gaps collapse into a recognizable spiraling silhouette even at 16–32px.
The dots disappear at the smallest sizes, but the arc pattern remains legible as a distinct shape.
Concept 2's single curve becomes a meaningless stroke at favicon scale. Concepts 3 and 4 lose
structural clarity — the constellation's fine lines vanish; the C becomes indistinguishable from
a generic letter.

**Clarity of metaphor at first glance**
The orbital paths read immediately as multiple agents in motion around a central hub — exactly the
Concerto product metaphor (one customer/operator, many orchestrated Claude Code sessions). Each dot
represents an agent running in its own orbit. The gap stagger suggests continuous, asymmetric
motion rather than static geometry.

**Anthropic-adjacency without copying**
The warm peach (#d97757) + violet (#8b7fff) palette is harmonious with claude.ai's dark-mode
aesthetic. The concentric-arc motif echoes the Claude ecosystem's visual vocabulary without
reproducing the specific Anthropic logo composition. The staggered gaps (45°, 195°, 330° — not the
evenly spaced 120° of Anthropic's mark), varying arc spans (300°/280°/280°), and agent-dot accents
are compositionally distinct.

**Memorability**
The asymmetric stagger creates a slightly tilted, spiraling silhouette with implied directionality
and rotation. Unlike fully symmetric marks, the eye moves through it — you track from the inner
peach arc through to the outer violet arc. You'd recognize it on second viewing because the
specific rotation pattern sticks.

### Why the others didn't win
Concept 2 is beautiful but too minimal to survive small-size rendering. Concept 3 is evocative but
noisy — constellation patterns are also heavily overused across the AI product space in 2026.
Concept 4 is strong as a wordmark device but fails as a standalone mark; the C cannot be detached
from its letter meaning and doesn't work as a logomark at sub-48px sizes.
